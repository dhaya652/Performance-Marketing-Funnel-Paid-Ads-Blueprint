# Performance Marketing Funnel & Paid Ads Blueprint

**Working example:** boAt Lifestyle  
**Campaign:** Wireless audio & wearables acquisition campaign  
**Planning period:** 21 Nov – 20 Dec 2026

## Deliverables
- `strategy/Paid-Advertising-Strategy-Blueprint.pdf` — funnel, Meta creative plan, Google Search campaign structure, negatives, budget model, metrics, measurement and optimization.
- `data/Performance-Marketing-Ad-Copy-and-Metrics.xlsx` — editable ad copy matrix, Google Search structure, negative keyword starter list, funnel plan and metric model.
- CSV exports for ad copy, Google Search structure and negative keywords.

## Important assumptions
This is a proposed educational strategy, not an official brand campaign. The financial model uses illustrative assumptions: monthly media budget ₹3,00,000, CPC ₹15, CVR 2.5%, AOV ₹2,500, and one new customer per order. The 4.0x target ROAS and target CAC are planning goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Replace these values with actual ad-account, analytics and finance data.

Product claims, availability, prices, offers, shipping and warranty terms must be checked against current official listings before ads go live. Tracking, consent, attribution windows and event deduplication must be validated in the relevant ad platforms.
